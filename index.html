06

Adobe Photoshop, Lightroom, Illustrator and Canva for image finishing, color work, illustration and campaign assets.

Adobe Premiere Pro

Strength: Professional timeline editing, audio, color and export control.

Trade-off: Subscription cost and a steeper learning curve than simpler editors.

Adobe Photoshop

Strength: Deep compositing, retouching and pixel-level image control.

Trade-off: Can be heavy for quick designs and requires organized files for complex projects.

Lightroom

Strength: Fast batch photo organization, color correction and catalog workflows.

Trade-off: Less suited to detailed compositing or multi-layer graphic design.

Adobe Illustrator

Strength: Scalable logos, icons, illustrations and vector artwork.

Trade-off: Takes longer to master for photo-based work and can feel excessive for simple layouts.
